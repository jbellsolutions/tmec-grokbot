# Partner types: which trades, in what order, and why

The target is a trade that does not sell solar, has a customer list worth emailing, and whose customers already trust it in their home. The ranking below is judgement from how each trade relates to homeowners, not data. It is replaced by measured results per trade (replies, yeses, sends, signed sales) as the scoreboard gets them. The `trades-list-build` skill (Trades Outreach) and the `segment-list-build` skill (Dream 500) work down this list.

## Wave 0: trades Tommy already knows

Before any cold email, Tommy lists the trades he already works alongside or knows by name. Tommy doesn't contact them himself (Justin, 11 Nov). The Dream 100 bot drafts a message to each for his yes, and on his yes the bot sends it from his mailbox, then the one-pager. Those relationships already exist, so they come first. Names: OPEN (Tommy supplies them).

Their work and solar sit in the same conversation. | 3 | Pool builders, pool service and pool equipment | Pumps and heating run long hours. Owners are homeowners. Servicing gives them a repeat list | Retail pool shops may sell one-off to people outside the area. Check the list is customers, not walk-ins |

| 5 | Renovators (extensions, kitchens, bathrooms), not new-construction builders | Solar is decided at renovation. Past clients are high-value homeowners | Tommy won't do new-construction builder work (9 Nov). Volume and project builders are excluded |
| 6 | Plumbers | In homes constantly. Hot water decisions sit next to energy decisions | Some also sell hot water heat pumps or solar. Check the site |
| 7 | Landscapers, lawn and garden services, cleaners, gutter and window cleaners | Regular contact with homeowners. Panel cleaners know who already has solar, which is a battery conversation | Customers may trust them less on energy advice, so the "why I trust them" line matters |
| 8 | Insulation, window tinting, blinds and security screens, glaziers | Home-improvement customers who are already spending on comfort and running costs | Smaller lists. Same solar check |

## Excluded

| Who | Why |
|---|---|
| Electricians and electrical contractors | Electrical is TMEC's own trade, and most also sell solar. They are competitors, not partners |
| Anyone who installs solar, batteries or EV chargers | Competitors, and they have nothing to recommend |
| Lead-generation and quote-comparison companies | They sell shared leads, which is what this program replaces |
| Franchises controlled from head office | The local owner usually cannot say yes or send to the franchise's list |
| Any business whose site says it does not want unsolicited commercial email | Respect the statement. Record the reason |
| Anyone on the suppression list | Never contacted again |

## How the ranking changes

After each wave the scoreboard shows, for each trade: emails sent, replies, yeses, campaigns sent, enquiries, signed sales. Trades with no sends are UNKNOWN, not zero. The bots propose a new order to Tommy through TMEC Command. Tommy decides. Nothing is promised about how many partners or sales any trade will produce.
