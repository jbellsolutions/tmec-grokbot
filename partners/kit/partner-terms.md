# Campaign partner terms: draft, have it checked

**Draft — have it checked.** This is a plain-English starting point, not legal advice and not a signed contract. Have an accountant look at the fee, GST and invoicing clauses. Anything in [square brackets] is blank until Tommy decides. The partner bots never fill these in.

Lines marked **[check]** touch Australian rules that `policies/AU-COMPLIANCE.md` covers: the Spam Act 2003 (consent, sender details, unsubscribe), the Privacy Act (sharing a customer's details), and the Australian Consumer Law (no misleading claims). If TMEC is a signatory to the New Energy Tech Consumer Code, ask whether a partner's marketing falls under it. That is unverified.

## 1. The parties

- **TMEC Services** ("TMEC"): [legal entity name and ABN, from `context/owner.md`].
- **The Partner**: [partner's business name and ABN].

## 2. What the Partner does

1. Sends one email, written with TMEC, recommending TMEC for solar, batteries and EV chargers, to the Partner's own customers, from the Partner's own account.
2. Sends it on a day the Partner chooses and TMEC has been told of.
3. Forwards any customer reply that asks to be contacted to [TMEC partner inbox].

A second send to the same list needs both sides to agree.

## 3. What TMEC does

1. Writes the email and gives the Partner a link and a phone code that identify the Partner.
2. Contacts and looks after every customer who gets in touch, from quote to install. The Partner takes no part in the quote or the contract.
3. Records each sale that comes from the Partner's email and pays the fee in clause 5.
4. Helps the Partner send, if the Partner asks. TMEC never sends to the Partner's customers and never takes the Partner's list.

## 4. The Partner's list and consent **[check]**

1. The list is the Partner's. TMEC does not ask for it, receive it or store it.
2. The Partner confirms that everyone on the list has dealt with the Partner and expects to hear from them by email, and that the Partner is comfortable that a recommendation of another business fits that. **[Whether a customer's agreement to hear from the Partner covers a recommendation of TMEC is a question for the legal check.]**
3. The Partner sends from its own account with its own business name, contact details and a working unsubscribe, and removes anyone who opts out.
4. The Partner does not add addresses it bought or collected another way.
5. A customer's name and contact details pass to TMEC only when that customer asks the Partner or TMEC to contact them.

## 5. The fee

1. TMEC pays the Partner [$______] for each sale that comes from the Partner's email. (Tommy sets the figure.)
2. A "sale" is counted when [signed | installed | the customer has paid] (Tommy picks one), and after [cancellation or cooling-off period, if any].
3. A sale comes from the Partner's email if the customer used the Partner's link or phone code, replied to the Partner who forwarded it, or named the Partner, within [attribution window] of the Partner's send. If a person was already a TMEC contact before the send, TMEC decides in good faith whether it counts and tells the Partner why.
4. If a customer is tied to two partners, the first recorded enquiry counts.
5. TMEC pays [within ______ of the sale counting], by bank transfer, on the Partner's invoice. The Partner gives TMEC an ABN and invoice details. [GST treatment: accountant to confirm.]
6. If a sale is cancelled or refunded before the fee is paid, no fee is payable. [What happens if it is cancelled after payment: Tommy to decide.]
7. A change to the fee applies to later sales only. TMEC tells the Partner in writing first.
8. TMEC keeps a record of every sale and shares the counts and references with the Partner on request. TMEC's record decides a disagreement, after TMEC has talked it through with the Partner in good faith.

## 6. What the Partner says **[check]**

1. The Partner uses the email as written wherever it talks about TMEC, the link or the code. The Partner may change its own words.
2. The Partner does not state prices, savings, rebate amounts, install times or results, and does not promise any of them.
3. The Partner tells its customers truthfully why it recommends TMEC, and does not claim experience it does not have.
4. The Partner discloses that it is paid a fee if a customer goes ahead. [Recommended. Whether it is required is for the legal check.]

## 7. Air-con **[Tommy to confirm or delete]**

TMEC will quote only solar, batteries and EV chargers to customers who come from the Partner's email. If such a customer asks about air-con, TMEC refers them back to the Partner.

## 8. Exclusivity

Nothing in this arrangement stops the Partner recommending other businesses for other things. The enquiries from the Partner's email are TMEC's only and are not sold or shared with anyone else.

## 9. Privacy **[check]**

Each side handles personal information under the Privacy Act. TMEC uses a customer's details to contact and serve that customer and to keep the sales record. TMEC does not give them to anyone else. The Partner does not give TMEC anyone's details without that person's agreement.

## 10. No agency, no employment

The Partner is an independent business. It is not TMEC's employee, agent or sales representative, cannot quote or bind TMEC, and has no authority to make promises on TMEC's behalf.

## 11. Ending it

Either side may end the arrangement by written notice. Sales that came from sends made before the notice are still counted and paid under clause 5.
