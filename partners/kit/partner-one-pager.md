**TMEC Services: campaign partners**

## What it is

You recommend TMEC Services to your own customers in one email. We write the email. You change it so it sounds like you, and send it from your own account. When your customers get in touch, TMEC's team looks after them from there. If a customer goes ahead with TMEC, we pay you a fee.

It is a campaign, not a referral you have to remember. You pick the day. We help you send it if you'd like.

## What you get

- **A fee of $______ for each sale** that comes from your email, payable when ______ . (Tommy sets both. Blank until he does.)
- A ready-to-send email in your voice, with subject lines to choose from.
- A link and a phone code that tell us a customer came from you, so every sale is tied to you.
- Help sending it, if you want it: a walk-through call, and a test send to check the link.
- An update when your customers get in touch, and when a sale you sent is confirmed. Counts and references, not your customers' private details.

## How it works

1. We agree a day for your send. You choose.
2. We give you the email, the subject lines, your link and your phone code.
3. You read it, change your own words, and send it to your customers from your account.
4. Your customers click the link, ring TMEC with your code, or reply "yes" to you. You forward any "yes" to TMEC.
5. TMEC's team follows up with each person. We tell you when a sale you sent is confirmed, and we pay your fee.

## What we ask of you

- Your list stays yours. We never ask for it and never take it.
- Send only to customers who expect to hear from you, from your own account, with your own business details and a working unsubscribe. Take off anyone who has opted out.
- Keep to the email as written where it talks about TMEC, the link and the code. Don't add prices, savings, rebate amounts or times. TMEC deals with all of that directly.
- Tell your customers truthfully why you're recommending us. We'll help you word it.

## What you don't have to do

Quote. Sell. Install. Chase anyone. Handle questions about prices, rebates or timing. That's TMEC's job.

A Sunshine Coast business installing solar, batteries and EV chargers, and doing electrical and air-con work. The installers are TMEC's own team, not subcontractors.
